# SYSTIVRA — English Google Play listing

**App name**: SYSTIVRA

**Short description** (under 80 characters):  
Monitor Android hardware, record device metrics, and export local reports.

**Full description**

SYSTIVRA gives you a clear view of the device information Android makes available, with a premium dark design and an English interface.

Explore battery charge and charging state, available memory and internal storage, Android version and device details, supported sensors, and platform-reported thermal information. Where supported, view live hardware measurements.

Start a recording when you choose. SYSTIVRA keeps your recordings in private local storage so you can review device activity and measured battery and temperature trends. Organize sessions, remove unwanted records, or save CSV and JSON reports with Android's file picker.

Use user-confirmed display, touch and vibration checks. Inspect CPU capabilities and OpenGL graphics-driver information where available.

No sign-in or account is needed. The core app does not request Internet permission. Optional Android Usage Access can display local app activity after you grant access in system settings.

The data provided by Android varies across hardware and Android versions. Unavailable measurements are clearly identified, not fabricated.
