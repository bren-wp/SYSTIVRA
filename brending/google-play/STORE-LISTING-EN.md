**App name:** SYSTIVRA

**Short description (74/80 characters):**  
Monitor Android hardware, record device metrics, and export local reports.

## Full description

SYSTIVRA lets you understand the information your Android device makes available, with clear readings and a clean, dark interface.

Explore battery level and charging status, available memory, internal storage, Android version, device information, supported sensors, and platform-reported thermal status. View CPU architecture and OpenGL graphics-driver information where supported.

Choose when to start recording device readings. Organize recordings into local sessions, review battery and temperature trends, and export measurements as CSV or JSON reports using Android's document picker. Delete saved sessions whenever you want.

Use guided, user-confirmed display, touch, and vibration checks. Try a short local CPU workload test with cancellation and a thermal safety cut-off.

No account or sign-in is required. SYSTIVRA's core features work without Internet permission. Optional Android Usage Access can display app activity locally after you explicitly enable it in system settings.

Available measurements depend on your device and Android version. Restricted or unsupported values are clearly marked as unavailable, rather than estimated.

SYSTIVRA by BRENDIGO.
