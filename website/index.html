ABOUT SYSTIVRA

Purpose-built for truthful diagnostics.

Android devices expose different levels of hardware detail. SYSTIVRA is being developed to show exactly what a device can report — and to explain what it cannot.

This is an open-development project. Feature availability, beta builds, benchmarks and long-term capture will be disclosed as they become validated.
